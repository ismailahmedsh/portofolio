# Pillars v2: the latest 100 pillars

Status as of 2026-11-07. Machine-readable copy: `config/pillars_v2.json`. This set went through four audit rounds and three reviewers (details below).

## The goal

Invest the owner's 2,500 AED a month (about US$679 after the FX minimum) for 15 years or more in low-cost Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs on the London Stock Exchange, with VWRA (the whole world) as the default and the yardstick for every alternative. Money moves away from VWRA only when a rule run exactly as it would be live has beaten simply holding the world after all costs, and holdings are sold only under the written sell rules, never because of price trends, scores or news mood.

## Where this stands, in plain terms

- Today every deposit goes to VWRA. No tilt or owner policy has yet passed the evidence tests (REC04 for scores, REC07 for owner policy weights).
- The 100 pillars feed three separate views that are never added together: which fund line to buy inside a sleeve (line view), whether an equity sleeve deserves a tilt against VWRA (exposure score X), and what a purchase does to the owner's actual holdings (fit view).
- Most pillars are gates, hard limits, context or records kept for later testing. Any pillar whose data source is not verified has weight 0.
- These are not yet proven to be the best 100. Three things will settle it: the REC03 redundancy check, the REC04 exact-rule backtest with a holdout, and 36 months of live record.

## The families

Weights are points within the view each family belongs to. The line view adds to 100 (CST 53, TAX 21, DUR 19, line events 7). The exposure score X adds to 100 (EXR 20, FAC 40, RSK 40), with VWRA = 50. The fit view is its own 100 points. A weight of 0 means gate, limit, context or record only.

## Scoring method

1. Instrument. Line pillars are computed on the LSE USD UCITS line. Exposure pillars are computed on each equity sleeve's reference index (the reference line, spliced under ELG07; ^NDX before QQQ), never on its best line.

2. Gates first: ELG01-ELG05, ELG08, ELG09, DUR02 (ETCs), FIT11, CON04 above 5%, CON05 flag 1, EVT01/02/05 at severity 2 or more, and EVT07 at severity 3. A failing line is still scored and reported but cannot receive money. Gates fail closed for buying and open for selling. VWRA is exempt from ELG08 and ELG09.

3. Transform. s = 50 + 50 x tanh(d x (x - ref) / k), in natural units, never universe percentiles. Pre-registered k scales live in config/pillar_scales.yaml (to be created):
- costs: TER 10 bps; tracking beyond TER 15; stability 15; noise 50; withholding 15; distribution 15; tax-rule dependence 25;
- residue 10%; traded value and size 1 log10 step; age 3 years; substitutes 1;
- currency z 1.0; reversal 20 points;
- factor betas 0.2;
- raw drawdown 10 points; time under water 24 months; stress 5 points; asymmetry 0.2; CVaR/vol 0.5;
- alpha 1% a year; down-market correlation 0.2; diversification return 10 bps; tracking error 0.5%.
These are also frozen under the config hash before the first REC04 run: the thresholds (55/15, 2.5/5 hysteresis), the bands, the EXR02 b_j priors, ERP_world 4%, r_forgone 0.6% a month, the execution ladder and the weights below. Any later change is a new trial (N_TRIALS + 1), logged in CHANGELOG.

4. One beta and one factor set.
- Every beta in the system (EXR02, FIT04, RSK01 stress, RSK04, RSK07) is the FAC05 univariate 120-month Ledoit-Wolf beta on the single ELG07 world reference.
- Every factor loading (FAC01-FAC04) comes from one common set: Ken French Developed 5 factors plus Developed momentum, with CMA orthogonalised to HML. It is applied to every equity sleeve and to VWRA. EIMI on Developed factors is a disclosed approximation, with an Emerging-set sensitivity run in REC04.

5. Three separate views. No cross-view composite.
- Line view (CST 53, TAX 21, DUR 19, line EVT 7 = 100), compared with the sleeve's median eligible line and ranked within the sleeve. Single-line sleeves use absolute anchors (TER 10 bps, AUM US$1bn). CST10, with weight 0, picks the line.
- Exposure view X (EXR 20, FAC 40, RSK 40 = 100), compared with VWRA = 50, for equity sleeves only.
- Fit view (FIT = 100), compared with the current portfolio (core only until positions exist).

6. One-sided pillars score s = 50 - 50 x tanh(max(0, d x (x - ref)) / k) and never exceed 50. They are RSK01 (and its components RSK02 and RSK04), RSK03, RSK05, FIT10, TAX03, DUR03 and line EVT. Calm earns nothing.

7. Weights inside families.
- CST: CST01 0.30, CST02 0.35, CST03 0.10, CST04 0.05, CST06 0.15, CST08 0.05. CST09 takes 0.10 from CST06 after 3 fills. CST05 and CST07 are 0.
- TAX: TAX01 0.4, TAX03 0.4, TAX05 0.2, when CST02 is missing for the sleeve. When CST02 exists, TAX01 is inactive by design and TAX03 and TAX05 are renormalised to 2/3 and 1/3. This is not treated as missing, so the shrink factor c of rule 10 is not applied. TAX02 and TAX04 are 0.
- DUR: DUR01 0.4, DUR04 0.3, DUR06 0.3. DUR03, DUR05, DUR07 and DUR08 are 0.
- Line EVT: EVT01, EVT02 and EVT05 equal.
- EXR: EXR06 0.4 (8% of X), EXR08 0.6 (12% of X).
- FAC: FAC01 0.12 (4.8% of X), FAC02 0.30, FAC03 0.28 (orthogonalised CMA, outside the value cluster), FAC04 0.30.
- RSK: RSK01 0.35 (14% of X), RSK03 0.35, RSK05 0.30.
- FIT for equity sleeves: FIT04 0.40, FIT05 0.20, FIT06 0.20, FIT10 0.20. For gold and bond sleeves: FIT05, FIT06 and FIT10 at 1/3 each, with FIT04 as context.
Caps: the value cluster EXR06 + EXR08 + FAC01 = 24.8% of X (cap 25%). The drawdown cluster = 14% of X (cap 15%).

8. Weight 0 (record, context, constraint, gate or derived):
- all ELG, DUR02, CON01-CON05;
- CST05, CST07, CST09 (below 3 fills), CST10;
- TAX02, TAX04; DUR03, DUR05, DUR07, DUR08;
- EXR01, EXR02, EXR03, EXR04, EXR05, EXR07, EXR09, EXR12, and EXR10 and EXR11 as context;
- FAC05; RSK02 and RSK04 on their own; RSK06-RSK08;
- FIT01-FIT03, FIT07-FIT09, FIT11;
- all MOM, REC and MAC;
- EVT03, EVT04 and EVT06-EVT10.

9. Verification rule. A pillar with an unverified source has weight 0 and is recorded. It is promoted when its source passes a probe, logged in CHANGELOG. Every FRED-dependent pillar uses curl --http1.1, then ECB data-api, BoE IADB or BIS where an equivalent exists, then the cached last good value with an age flag (stale beyond 45 days reads missing). Shiller uses the cached last good wsimg link. Holdings stand-ins are verified sources used for constraints only, disclosed, and reconciled by ELG10. No paid sources.

10. Missing data, applied the same way in every view.
- If the reference (VWRA, or any eligible line in the sleeve) lacks a pillar, that pillar is missing for everything compared with it.
- Otherwise a missing pillar drops out, and the family score is 50 + c x (mean - 50), where c is the share of the family's weight that has data.
- Nothing is filled with 50. ELG09 counts only verified pillars. Missing pillars are listed weekly.

11. Point-in-time rules:
- stress windows count only once they have ended at least 1 month before t;
- EXR06 needs at least 10 years of REER data and 70% coverage;
- REC04 uses dated frozen region mixes;
- Ken French, BIS and FRED values use release lags, and their revised vintages are disclosed;
- CMA_perp is orthogonalised on an expanding window;
- one world reference (ELG07) is used everywhere.

12. Smoothing. Monthly line decisions use the median of 4 weekly readings. X used for targets is the mean of the last 3 month-end values, identical live and in REC04, with two consecutive qualifying quarters. Weekly X is display only.

13. Built-in checks. The run fails loudly if any of these break:
- VWRA scores exactly 50 on every X family, and VWRA's loadings minus VWRA's = 0 on every factor;
- with all tilts at 0 and equal costs, FIT04 = 0 ± 0.1% for every equity exposure (it holds identically with one beta);
- every money-market, T-bill and ultra-short line fails ELG04;
- no low-volatility fund scores above 50 on a one-sided pillar;
- CST10 reproduces SPXS -27.65 and VUAA -16.9 bps on the iShares-style branch with seeded ter_history (test fixture), and on the fail-closed branch, which applies today, ranks SPXS at about 5 bps (TER 5 + US withholding about 0 for a swap line) against VUAA at about 26 bps (TER 7 + about 19);
- the batching rule gives k = 1 at F = US$2 and k = 2 at F = US$10;
- a REC04 run dated 2015-01 sees only the 2007-09 stress window (plus 2011 in RSK06);
- dsr() is run on the active-return series;
- the config hash matches the one REC04 tested.
Before launch, REC03 on the REC04 panel must show no scored pair with |rho| of 0.85 or more (merge otherwise), and EXR06/EXR08, EXR08/FAC01 and FAC01/FAC03 below 0.7.

## Allocation method for the monthly 2,500 AED

Three layers. The pool and its limits are policy. Targets come from owner policy that has passed REC07, or from scores after REC04 passes. Routing happens monthly; the weekly run never moves money.

1. THE POOL. Sleeve lines were verified on yfinance as USD LSE lines on 2026-11-07. Universe edits are pending before launch: add EXUS, ACWD and SPYY; remove EQAC and the duplicate CUSS, R2US, WSML and XDEW rows; move XDEW to us/equal_weight; set the VWRA TER to 0.14; add ter_history seeded from annual reports.
- Core, world: VWRA, fixed as the designated line. CST10 never re-chooses it; it changes only under S1 or S2. Fallback chain: FWRA, then ACWD, each only once it has an owner-checked ELG08 entry. Until then the chain is VWRA only.
- S&P 500: SPXS (US$14.95), VUAA (US$143.90), CSPX (US$802).
- Nasdaq-100: XNAS (US$63.16), CNDX (US$1,563), EQQU (US$665, distributing).
- Developed ex-US: EXUS (US$44.75).
- Emerging markets: EIMI.
- Factor and small-cap: IWVL, IWQU, WSML. Excluded from score mode unless the ELG07 splice passes.
- Gold: IGLN. Owner-sized at 0 or 5%, never score-targeted.
- Diversifier bonds: owner share D of 0-20%, default 0, never score-targeted.
- Limits:
  - Any satellite is 0% or at least 5%.
  - At most 4 lines in total, counting frozen old lines (FIT11).
  - While REC04 fails, equity satellite policy_targets sum to at most 0.15 of the equity portion (1 - D - gold).
  - Core = equity portion minus equity satellites. For example, D = 10% and gold = 5% give a core of at least 0.85 x 0.85 = 72.25% of the portfolio.
- Flow cap: equity satellites receive at most 2 of any 12 consecutive deposits. This is the effective form of satellite_share 0.20 (16.7% of dollars), hence the 0.15 cap. The gold and diversifier sleeves are not under this cap. They are filled by gap routing up to their owner targets, so raising D is cured by deposits first, and S5 rebalances at most once a year.

2. TARGETS.
(a) Before REC04 passes (now). X is computed, logged and reported but sets no targets. Equity satellite targets come only from owner policy_targets in config/portfolio.yaml. The default is none, and satellite_share = 0, so every deposit goes to VWRA.
A policy routes money only if all of these hold:
- the hard constraints pass: FIT07, FIT08, FIT09, FIT10 at 3% or less, RSK06 and RSK07;
- REC07, built to its full definition at MONTHLY = 681, has been re-run for this exact policy;
- it beats a VWRA-only portfolio by at least 0.25 points a year money-weighted in every period under both timings, with a deflated information-ratio probability above 0.95 counting every policy version in REC07_TRIALS (added to N_TRIALS and logged in CHANGELOG.md).
The round-2 CHANGELOG-override route is removed, because it conflicted with GOAL.md's evidence rule. The preliminary REC07 run lost in 2003-07, so no policy currently qualifies. Allowing owner-signed regime bets would need the owner to amend GOAL.md explicitly; the system does not offer it. Before the first policy deposit, a CHANGELOG entry must also amend DECISION_RULES rule 2: the destination changes from 'top-ranked ETF' to 'policy-target gap', and the cap becomes 2 of any 12 deposits.
(b) After REC04 passes. The owner may set target_mode: score. Equity sleeves only.
- X is the mean of the last 3 month-end X values on the sleeve's reference index (the same definition REC04 tests). Weekly X is display only.
- A sleeve qualifies when X is 55 or more at two consecutive quarterly reviews and FIT04 is above 0.
- Target = band max x clip((X - 55) / 15, 0, 1), rounded to 0 or at least 5%, then scaled down until every constraint holds.
- Hysteresis: each change is at least 2.5 and at most 5 points per sleeve per quarter. The owner confirms each change.

3. MONTHLY DEPOSIT.
(a) Funding. The owner records in config/portfolio.yaml the funding method (IBKR AED conversion, or a bank AED-to-USD conversion with a USD wire), its fees and checked_on. Let F be the fixed USD cost per conversion and r_forgone = 0.6% a month, the expected nominal equity return given up while AED waits at the bank (the same constant as in CST10). Choose k in {1, 2} to minimise F/k + 1.70 x orders/k + ((k-1)/2) x 680.74 x r_forgone. k is capped at 2, so batched AED waits at the bank for at most one month and IBKR cash is never idle longer than one month.
- F = US$2: k=1 costs 3.70, k=2 costs 1 + 0.85 + 2.04 = 3.89, so k = 1.
- F = US$10: k=1 costs 11.70, k=2 costs 5 + 0.85 + 2.04 = 7.89, so k = 2.
A unit test covers both. The same batching is applied in REC04 and REC07.
This month: 2,500 / 3.6725 = US$680.74, minus US$2 = US$678.74. C = that plus any residue.
(b) Gaps. g = target x (portfolio value + C) - sleeve value (FIT01). If positions are missing or stale, or no gap exceeds US$100, everything goes to the core.
(c) Routing. All of C goes in one order to the largest positive gap. An equity satellite may receive it only if the 2-of-12 flow cap still holds; otherwise the core gets it. A second order is possible only when C is at least US$680 (k = 2 batches), with both orders at least US$340. Never more than 2 orders.
(d) Line choice (satellites). The line must pass:
- ELG01-ELG05, ELG08, ELG09, CON04 and CON05;
- FIT11;
- no EVT01, EVT02 or EVT05 event at severity 2 or more, and no EVT07 event at severity 3;
- a line score of 40 or more.
Buy the lowest CST10. The new-money line changes only when a challenger is at least 5 bps a year cheaper at 2 consecutive monthly runs (each run uses the median of 4 weekly readings). The challenger is the designated replacement and exempt from FIT11's duplicate test. The old line is frozen and still counts toward the 4-line limit; if the switch would make a fifth line, there is no switch and S2 governs.
(e) Whole shares: buy floor((order - 1.70) / price). If the cheapest line does not fit, use the next line within 10 bps a year all-in; otherwise route to the next gap.
(f) Fallback. If no line in the sleeve passes, go to the next sleeve. If none passes, buy the core chain, VWRA first. FWRA and ACWD are used only with owner-checked ELG08 entries and with ELG01, ELG02 and ELG05 passing and EVT01 below severity 2.
(g) Execution. A deterministic ladder stored in config, with every rung logged in REC05 and CST09. Orders go in on the EVT09 day, 14:30-16:15 London.
- Rung 1, session 1: a limit at mid plus a quarter of the spread (mid under MAC03 stress).
- Rung 2, sessions 2-3: re-enter at mid plus half the spread. If unfilled at 16:20, cancel and re-enter the next session.
- Rung 3, session 4: a marketable limit at the ask.
Never skip the deposit.
(h) In a drawdown nothing changes: new money fills whatever fell.

Worked example (illustrative only: it assumes a future REC07 pass for policy_targets S&P 500 = 0.10, D = 0, gold = 0). The owner holds VWRA US$20,000. C = US$678.74.

- This is the first satellite deposit in the last 12, so the 2-of-12 cap allows it.
- Line choice by CST10: today the S&P 500 sleeve fails closed (no seeded fee history for Invesco or Vanguard), so it ranks by current fee plus US withholding: SPXS about 5 bps against VUAA about 26 bps, so SPXS once it has led for 2 monthly runs, subject to TAX03 (its edge depends on the US section 871(m) swap rule). With full fee history the formula would give SPXS -27.65 against VUAA -16.9 bps, the same order.
- Order: 45 SPXS x US$14.95 = US$672.75, plus US$1.70 commission; residue US$4.29.
- Next month the satellite may take a second deposit. A third within 12 months goes to the core.

## Sell rules

Default: hold, and steer with new money. Deposits fix drift before any sale. Every sale is logged with its rule, sources, cost and the hold counterfactual (REC06).

S0. Never a reason to sell:
- price trend (MOM04), momentum, any score or rank, or X;
- drawdown depth (RSK08), volatility or any MAC reading;
- news sentiment, the EVT03 verdict, or EVT09 crisis status.
Evidence: trend-selling the core cost 1-3 points a year, and score rotation lost 2-10 points a year in the backtest. At -20% on the core: report it and keep depositing.

S1. Structural exit of a line. Act within one month of primary-source confirmation. Triggers:
- EVT01 at severity 2 or more;
- a gate turning to fail: ELG01 including DUR02 custody, ELG02-ELG04, or ELG08 confirmed by the owner;
- a feed stale for more than 7 trading days (ELG05), confirmed;
- a benchmark change that takes the line out of its sleeve (EVT04);
- EVT05 or EVT07 events impairing 5% or more of NAV.
Action: switch the whole position to the sleeve's best eligible substitute (DUR06, owner-checked under ELG08, lowest CST10), never into cash.
Size rule: if the position exceeds 10% of the target line's CST06 63-session median daily traded value, split the switch across sessions, selling and buying the same amount each session (relevant for FWRA at about US$3.6m a day, and for EXUS).
Discount check, time-aligned with CST07's method:
- iShares lines: NAV(t) from the Historical NAVs sheet against the LSE close(t), adjusted by the reference index or futures move between the NAV valuation point and 16:30 London.
- VWRA has only the vanguard.co.uk embedded navPrices JSON spot value and no NAV history. It uses the no-NAV branch, as do SPXS and XNAS.
- If the aligned discount is more than 2% below NAV, wait until it is below 1%, or for at most 60 days.
- No-NAV branch: wait up to 5 sessions, then switch.
A merger into a line that passes every gate may be accepted.

S2. Cost or durability switch. First stop buying and send new money to the substitute. Sell and switch only if all three hold:
- (a) the held line's CST10 exceeds the best eligible same-index line's by 15 bps a year or more at 2 consecutive quarterly checks;
- (b) the annual saving x 10 years exceeds 3 times the round trip (2 x US$1.70, both half-spreads, any FX);
- (c) less than half of the saving depends on one tax rule (TAX03, measured beyond TER).
G must rest on at least 3 years of same-index closes restated with a complete ter_history (CST02 fails closed otherwise). No core switch (VWRA to FWRA) is considered until ter_history covers VWRA's TER cuts and 2 quarters have passed since the July 2026 cut. The S1 size rule applies.
Durability works the same way. A flag (fund AUM under US$100m with 12-month outflows once DUR03 has 52 real snapshots, or EVT05 severity 1) stops buying. A switch follows only if the flag holds for 2 monthly runs and a substitute exists.

S3. Band or constraint breach from drift. Applies when a sleeve is more than 5 points above its band max or its policy target plus 5, or actual weights breach FIT07-FIT10, RSK06 or RSK07. First stop buying the sleeve. Sell only if FIT02 shows more than 12 months of deposits are needed to cure it. Then sell down to the limit (not to target) in one order, at most once a quarter, and buy the most underweight sleeve in the same session.

S4. Satellite removal. Triggers:
- the owner sets its policy target to 0;
- the quarterly REC07 re-run no longer passes the REC07 gate (0.25-point margin in every period under both timings, deflated probability above 0.95);
- in score mode, X below 50 for 2 quarters or FIT04 at 0 or below;
- EVT07 severity 3 lasting more than a quarter.
The sleeve is then frozen: no new money and no sale, and deposits dilute it. It is sold only if still above 5% after 12 months, or under S1-S3. A frozen sleeve under 2% may be merged into the core at a quarterly review.

S5. Diversifier rebalance, only if D is above 0. Once a year, return to D if it is more than 5 points off. Selling equity to buy bonds is forbidden while the world is more than 20% below its 52-week high.

S6. Tax or legal change (EVT06 severity 2 or more): freeze all sales until an adviser review and a re-run of the TAX pillars.

Guardrails for S2-S5:
- a 30-day cooling-off and a re-run of the scores;
- no non-structural sale while the world is more than 15% below its 52-week high;
- missing, stale-cached or unverified data never triggers a sale: gates fail closed for buying and open for selling;
- the core's designated line is sold only under S1, or under S2 after the conditions above.
