# ETF pillars v3 (round 2)

Written 2026-11-13 from config/pillars_v3.json. This is the plain-language owner version of the 100 pillars.

## The goal

Each month, put the AED 2,500 deposit (about US$678.74 after FX) into the owner's chosen mix of world, S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and a 5% speculative bitcoin sleeve, on the cheapest safe line for a UAE resident, and act at once on structural events such as fund closures. Everything else, including news, macro and scores, is shown and logged every week but moves no money until it beats the owner's mix in a pre-registered live test.

Owner instruction this version follows: "go, bitcoin in at 5%, options-income in, 60k cap fine".

## Why v3 looks the way it does

Pillars v3 (round 2) merges the five designs into one set of exactly 100 pillars. It meets the owner requirements of 2026-11-13 and the owner's relayed instruction: "go, bitcoin in at 5%, options-income in, 60k cap fine". This revision fixes the two blocking problems and the other audit findings from round 1.

Split (target 25/10/20/25/10/10, met exactly):
- news and events: 25 (NWS01-NWS17 market news, plus 8 structural EVT pillars). All 25 now score each mapped fund group or sleeve from -2 to +2 with dated sources, logged weekly in runs/news_log.csv. The EVT pillars also keep their 0-3 severity, which is the gate and S1 trigger. EVT09's group score is an execution-window score (only -1 or 0). It is logged but kept out of the news view N and out of the primary REC08 test, because it forecasts order-day friction, not 1-3 month returns. That is the one stated nuance on requirement 1.
- macro regime: 10 (MAC01-MAC10, all computed by code);
- line choice and safety: 20 (ELG 8, CST 6, TAX 3, DUR 3, down from 33 by merging near-duplicates);
- exposure, valuation and risk: 25 (EXR 6, FAC 6, RSK 5, CON 3, OPT 4, BTC 1);
- fit with the owner's mix: 10 (FIT);
- record and testing: 10 (REC).

US universe count, a small stated deviation from "about 150": round 2 adds four products to the information-only list. They are BTCI (bitcoin-linked, so it would bypass the 5% bitcoin cap), SVOL (short VIX futures), YMAX (a fund of single-stock option-income ETFs) and ULTY (option income on a few single stocks). That leaves 145 US-listed buy candidates (168 unique US tickers minus 23 information-only). It is still about 150, and the four would break owner requirement 4 if they were buyable.

What does not change:
- The owner's strategic mix (config/portfolio_v2.yaml) routes every deposit to the sleeve furthest below target, and it is the yardstick.
- Structural events act at once.
- Scores and news move no deposit money until a pre-registered rule beats the mix in the gate (DECISION_RULES_V2 section 2). Scores use REC04. News uses REC08's single primary test first, then the REC04 standard.

The evidence all designs agree on:
- Rotation lost to holding. In runs/backtest_latest.json (2015-2026, monthly deposits), holding VT returned 11.85% money-weighted. Weekly top-5 returned 5.71%, monthly top-5 8.68% and weekly top-1 2.79%. Momentum switching returned 14.42%, but with a 49.7% drawdown against 32.6% for holding, and it lost about 6% a year in 2015-2019.
- REC04: score tilts lose to the v2 mix (information ratio -0.24 and -0.21, deflated probability 0.00-0.001). This result was measured against the 70/20/10 mix and must be re-run against the new 66.5/19/9.5/5 mix and the cross-wrapper groups before v3 cites it.
- REC07: the 70/20/10 mix returned 11.2% against 9.9% for 100% world (2003-2026), with a similar worst drop.
- Macro predictors fail out of sample (Welch and Goyal 2008). Media tone moves prices briefly and then partly reverses (Tetlock 2007).  
So macro is context and guardrails only. News is a large, logged, forward-tested category, never a backtested decision driver.

Main choices:  
1. Same-index groups span wrappers (ELG07) and count once.
   - Membership comes from declared benchmark identity, not from a return correlation. Measured on 2026-11-13, weekly correlations across London and New York stay below 0.98 even for identical indices (CSPX.L/VOO 0.93), and even for two share classes of one fund on one exchange (JEPG.L/JEGA.L 0.89). Monthly correlations pass 0.95 (CSPX.L/VOO 0.965, VWRA.L/VT 0.965, CNDX.L/QQQ 0.982, JEPQ.L/JEPQ 0.985, JEPI.L/JEPI 0.976, BTCW.L/IBIT 0.993).
   - The return check is therefore monthly with a threshold of 0.95 across venues. Failing it flags a line for review but never splits a benchmark-identical group.  
2. One line-choice formula across wrappers (CST10).
   - US-listed lines always carry 30% investor withholding on ordinary distributions (no US-UAE treaty; IRC 871(a), 1441). Irish UCITS carry the 15% look-through inside the fund.
   - When CST02 is missing, every group uses the fallback TERnow + TAX01 + entry cost. Nothing fails closed for lack of ter_history.
   - The comparisons now use true same-strategy twins: VOO about 35 bps against CSPX 23.5; VT about 53 against VWRA 24.6; QQQM about 29 against XNAS 33.4 (inside the hurdle); JEPI about 273 against JEPI.L about 55; JEPQ about 394 against JEPQ.L about 45.
   - The UCITS figures are TER plus the 15% look-through on an assumed stock dividend yield of about 1.5% for JEPI.L and 0.7% for JEPQ.L. ELN coupons are assumed to carry no US withholding.  
3. The US$60,000 US-situs cap (FIT12, S8) has bands at 45k and 54k and a switch at 60k. It fails closed for buying: no US-situs order until ibkr_entity, w8ben_signed_on and positions_as_of are recorded.  
4. Bitcoin: speculative, owner target 5%, and 5% is now a true cap.
   - Buys never take B above 5%.
   - At every month-end check, once the deposit has been routed elsewhere, B above 5.0% is trimmed to 4.5% (S7). The trim is always at least 0.5% of the portfolio, which keeps its commission share falling as the portfolio grows.
   - Bitcoin orders need at least US$340, so the US$1.70 commission stays at or below 0.5%. A smaller gap is carried. Below a portfolio of about US$6,800 there is no bitcoin buy.
   - The owner may choose a 4.5% target instead to reduce churn; the cap stays 5%.
   - The preferred line is a physically backed LSE ETP. These are secured ETNs, not ETFs. They are not US-situs, but they need the owner's crypto-ETP permission and a TER seeded from the KID before any order. If they cannot be ordered, the sleeve uses a US spot ETF inside FIT12 headroom.  
5. Options-income (the O view) is rescaled so it is no longer saturated.
   - OPT01 scores upside given up against the plain buy-write of the same underlying (BXM 0.56, BXN 0.44) and against the line's own beta. Measured: JEPQ up-capture 0.73 at beta 0.66; QYLD 0.52 at beta 0.51. JEPQ scores about 84 and QYLD about 59.
   - OPT02 scores net income against peers on the same underlying, not against the index dividend yield.
   - OPT03 uses beta-adjusted down-capture plus the beta-matched net return gap.
   - The gate is now that a line's O must be at least the O of the plain buy-write proxy.
   - The sleeve target stays 0 until the owner answers an explicit prompt: target (10% proposed), underlying, and which sleeve it comes from. REC07 variant (b) shows the cost beside the prompt: BXM trailed the S&P 500 by 3.75 points a year and BXN trailed the Nasdaq-100 by 10.2.  
6. Never bought (information only): leveraged, inverse, single-stock (TSLY, NVDY, MSTY, CONY), concentrated single-stock baskets (ULTY, YMAX), buffer, volatility (SVOL), futures-crypto and bitcoin-linked income (BTCI), US partnerships and unsecured ETNs.  
7. News: 25 lead agents choose explorer counts above a floor and the number of rounds until saturation. Every weekly per-group score goes to a hash-chained ledger. Display only until the single pre-registered REC08 primary test passes.  
8. X keeps v2's weights exactly. Before v3 claims that the REC04 result still stands, REC04 and REC07 are re-run against the new yardstick mix and the cross-wrapper groups.

Source labels were corrected: FIT01, FIT12, FIT13, CON04, NWS14, REC03 and EXR06 are unverified until their inputs exist. MAC05 uses CNY=X in place of CNH=X, which returns no data. MAC04's term premium is labelled Kim-Wright. Cboe files are fetched with redirects followed.

## How the news agents work

There are 25 news pillars: NWS01-NWS17 (market news) and EVT01, EVT02 and EVT04-EVT09 (structural events). Each gets its own lead agent. This replaces v2's single planner, shared explorer pool and 10 one-shot event agents in .claude/workflows/weekly-etf-review-v2.js. The planner, explorer and synthesiser machinery is reused, organised per pillar.

1. Week pack (code, Friday after run_v2.py). v2/packs_v2.py writes runs/{week}/news_packs/{ID}.md for each pillar with:
- the pillar's scope and its fund groups from config/news_map.yaml (to create). The groups are the 28 buckets in config/buckets.txt plus bitcoin and the ELG07 options-income groups (by underlying and strategy). Each is mapped to ELG07 same-index groups and owner sleeves with frozen weights m. EVT08 also maps to the venue sets (LSE UCITS, US-listed, bitcoin ETP route, US bitcoin route);
- each group's 1-week and 4-week return against the mix, as a z-score on 52-week volatility; moves with |z| above 2 are marked unexplained;
- the held and shortlisted lines (UCITS, US-listed, LSE ETPs) and their groups;
- MAC01-MAC10 readings and any flag that turned this week;
- the EVT09 calendar for the window;
- last week's scores, open threads and watch flags;
- the pillar's source whitelist and primary sources.  
The window runs Saturday to Friday close of the ISO week. Older material is allowed only when labelled as background, and background never sets a score.

2. Lead agent per pillar. It reads the pack, runs 1-2 dated headline searches and chooses its own topics. It must seed a topic for each of these:  
(a) every unexplained group move;  
(b) every held or owner-mix group in its map, at least as a 'no material news' check;  
(c) every carried-over open thread or EVT watch flag;  
(d) every scheduled event in the window;  
(e) every MAC flag change mapped to it: MAC01, MAC04 and MAC08 to NWS01; MAC02 and MAC04 to NWS02; MAC05 to NWS11 and EVT08; MAC07 to NWS12 and NWS13.  
Some pillars have fixed checklists:
- EVT01 covers every held line and its same-index substitutes;
- EVT02 covers every blank TER to seed;
- EVT06 covers each listed tax rule;
- NWS15 and NWS16 cover the shortlisted bitcoin and options-income lines.  
Topics must be specific: a release, a decision, a company, a filing or a country event. The lead merges overlaps and sends one explorer per topic.  
Explorer count is the lead's decision above a floor. The floor is 1 + A, where A is an activity index from 0 to 4 scoring 1 point each for an unexplained move, a mapped MAC flag change, an open thread, and a held line in scope. The lead may raise the count to the cap of 6 per round. A quiet scope (A = 0) may run 1 explorer but must log 3 dated source checks. Each later round's count is again the lead's choice, up to 6.

3. Explorer rounds. Each explorer returns facts {claim, date, source, URL, verbatim quoted span, tier, fact or opinion}, the groups affected with a provisional score from -2 to +2, and new threads it could not cover. Tiers: T1 is a primary source; T2 is top-tier press (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, Nikkei); T3 is everything else.  
After each round the lead runs a saturation test. A pillar is saturated only when all four hold:  
(i) the last round changed no group score by 1 or more and no EVT severity;  
(ii) fewer than 20% of the round's facts are new distinct T1 or T2 facts;  
(iii) no open thread passes the relevance test, meaning none could change a group score by 1 or more, an EVT severity, a gate, FIT12 or FIT13;  
(iv) every mapped group has at least 2 dated sources or an explicit null check.  
If it is not saturated, the lead sends explorers only for the threads that pass (iii), with no repeats of topics already asked. The lead decides when to stop, within these caps:
- 4 rounds per pillar, or 5 for an EVT pillar holding an unconfirmed severity-2 candidate;
- 6 explorers per round and 18 per pillar;
- a lead may request one extra round beyond the cap, with a logged reason audited in REC09;
- a workflow budget of 450 agents, with priority to EVT pillars, then pillars touching held sleeves, then higher A.  
A pillar stopped by a cap is logged as unsaturated, its confidence is capped at low, and any score is capped at |1|. Every task skipped at the cap is logged.

4. Cross-pillar synthesiser, after each global round and at the end. It:
- de-duplicates facts by URL and event key, and gives each fact one owning pillar, so one event is scored once in N (the news equivalent of counting same-index lines once). Other pillars may reference it but not score it again;
- flags contradictions and names cross-topic chains, for example oil supply shock, then breakevens, then real yields, then the Nasdaq-100;
- routes threads to the pillar that owns them; structural facts found by NWS explorers go to the right EVT pillar;
- runs at most one adjudication round of up to 6 explorers.  
After synthesis, a blind verifier agent re-checks every +/-2 score and every EVT severity of 2 or more. The score survives only if the verifier finds no missing counter-fact and no unconfirmed source.

5. Scoring per fund group, for all 25 pillars on -2..+2.  
NWS leads score each mapped group for expected USD return over the next 1-3 months:
- most groups: against the world;
- options-income groups: against their underlying;
- global_equity and bitcoin: also an absolute score, logged only.  
Scores judge policy, fundamentals and flows, not price moves alone. 0 is the default, and most groups score 0 or +/-1 in most weeks. Rules:
- each score carries a confidence (high 1, medium 0.67, low 0.33), a rationale of 2-4 sentences, what would prove it wrong, and fact ids;
- |1| needs 2 independent dated sources inside the window;
- |2| needs a T1 source plus a T2 report and a concrete, material development;
- opinion never supports a 2, and T3 can open a thread but never supports a score alone;
- NWS14 uses its pre-registered contrarian sign.  
Calibration anchors sit in the prompt; for example, a 25 bp surprise cut is +1 for rate-sensitive groups, not +2.  
EVT leads write two outputs:  
(1) severity 0-3 per line or issuer. Severity 2 or more needs a T1 source with the quoted text. EVT02's cheaper-substitute trigger and EVT07's restricted-market rows are confirmed by code;  
(2) a group or sleeve score on -2..+2 under the same source and confidence rules, from the pre-registered map. Adverse severity 1, 2 or 3 gives -1, -2 or -2 for the groups whose held or CST10-chosen line, or whose whole exposure, is affected; EVT01 is one step less negative when a ready substitute exists. A confirmed favourable event gives +1: a fee cut, a cheaper same-index substitute, a new UCITS twin, an access improvement, lower index concentration, or a lifted sanction. A material favourable rule change confirmed by T1 plus T2 gives +2. Otherwise the score is 0. EVT09 scores only -1 or 0, for an order-window conflict.  
News view per group: N_g = 50 + 12.5 x sum_p(m_pg x c_pg x s_pg) / sum_p(m_pg x c_pg), over the 17 NWS pillars and EVT01, EVT02 and EVT04-EVT08; EVT09 is excluded as an execution score. N ranges from 25 to 75. Groups no pillar touched read 50 and are flagged 'not covered'. The 4-week mean of N is the one primary signal tested in REC08.

6. Code checks before anything is stored. Every URL is fetched (with redirects followed) and must return HTTP 200, its date must fall inside the window (otherwise it is relabelled background), and the verbatim quoted span must be present on the page. Facts that fail are dropped. A score left with fewer than 2 valid sources becomes 0 with low confidence. Paywalled facts need a second source, and social media counts only from official accounts. Before any severity of 2 or more opens a gate, S1, S6 or S8, code confirms the quoted primary-source text and the owner confirms the action. Agents never write numbers into scored config; TER seeds from EVT02 are code-checked against the KID before entering ter_history.

7. Logging. Each pillar writes:
- runs/{week}/news/{ID}.json: facts with quotes, group scores, severities, rounds, explorers, saturation, verifier result and agent count;
- a digest under 300 words in {ID}.md;
- for EVT pillars, also runs/{week}/events/{ID}.json.  
Code then appends rows to two files, both created before the first v3 run:
- runs/news_log.csv: one row per week, pillar and group, for all 25 pillars, with the REC08 fields;
- runs/news_facts.csv: fact id, URL, date, tier, verbatim quote, quote hash and owning pillar.  
Both are append-only and hash-chained, and they are frozen before Monday's LSE open, with the hash copied into plan_log.csv. A correction is a new row with a reason and is excluded from REC08's IC. REC09 audits sources, saturation, extra rounds and agreement between leads.

8. What news may do.
- EVT severity 2 or more blocks deposits to the affected lines at once, and opens S1, S6 or S8 after primary-source confirmation. EVT06 can update FIT12's cap after owner confirmation.
- NWS and EVT group scores and N are display only next to the line, X, O and fit views. They are never a reason to sell (S0), never size bitcoin or options-income, and never move deposits until REC08's primary test passes (104 weeks or more of live log), followed by the REC04 standard.
- There is no historical backtest of news, because agents know later outcomes.
- The weekly committee in weekly-etf-review-v2.js keeps its role, but cites ledger rows instead of free text. Leads may see holdings, for relevance, but not the owner's gains or losses.

## Scoring method

1. Instrument and grouping. Line pillars (ELG, CST, TAX, DUR, line EVT) are computed on the exact traded line: an LSE USD UCITS line, a US-listed line or an LSE bitcoin ETP. Every line belongs to one ELG07 group, keyed on declared benchmark identity across wrappers and confirmed by a monthly return check (0.95 across venues, 0.98 within one exchange). X, N, O and the fit view are computed once per group on its reference series, spliced under ELG07. Every count, IC, redundancy test and REC04/REC08 trial count treats a group as one. Same-index lines differ only in the line view.

2. Gates first. A failing line is still scored and shown, but cannot receive money. Gates fail closed for buying and open for selling, and VWRA is exempt from ELG08 and ELG09. The gates:
- ELG01-ELG05, ELG07-ELG09 (a blank TER fails ELG09);
- DUR02 for gold ETCs, bitcoin products and the ELN counterparty limit;
- CON04 above 5% (missing until its file exists), and CON05 flag 1;
- FIT07 and FIT08 (for tilts), FIT11, FIT12 (red band, or missing entity, W-8BEN or positions_as_of for a US-situs order), FIT13 (5% cap, US$340 minimum);
- EVT01, EVT02 or EVT05 at severity 2 or more, and EVT07 at severity 3;
- for an options-income line, O below the O of its plain buy-write proxy.  
Information-only types (ELG03) are shown in a separate watch table.

3. Transform (unchanged from v2): s = 50 + 50 x tanh(d x (x - ref) / k), in natural units, never universe percentiles. One-sided pillars use s = 50 - 50 x tanh(max(0, d x (x - ref)) / k) and never exceed 50: RSK01 (each component), RSK03, RSK05, FIT10, TAX03, DUR01's flow part and line EVT. The k scales are frozen in config/pillar_scales.yaml. New scales:
- TAX01 for US-listed lines: k = 15 bps;
- OPT01(a): capture against the buy-write proxy, k = 0.30; OPT01(b): up-capture minus beta, k = 0.10;
- OPT02: net income against the peer median, k = 3 points;
- OPT03(a): down-capture minus beta, k = 0.10; OPT03(b): beta-matched net gap, k = 2 points;
- news scores: linear, capped at +/-2.

4. One beta (FAC05; options-income lines also get a beta to their underlying) and one factor set (Ken French Developed 5 factors plus momentum, CMA orthogonalised to HML), as in v2.

5. Five views, with no cross-view composite:
- Line view, per same-index group: CST 50, TAX 24, DUR 19, EVT 7 = 100. Lines are ranked against the group's median eligible line; single-line groups use absolute anchors (TER 10 bps, AUM US$1bn). CST10, at weight 0, picks the line, using its fallback (TERnow + TAX01 + entry) wherever CST02 is missing.
- Exposure view X, per equity group, with the world = 50: EXR 20, FAC 40, RSK 40, identical to v2. EXR06 is missing (not built) until its probe passes, so EXR is scored on EXR08 with coverage c. REC04 must be re-run in this state against the new mix before its result is cited for v3. Bitcoin is excluded. Options-income groups get X on their own history, for information only.
- Options-income view O, per options-income line: OPT01 0.40, OPT02 0.25, OPT03 0.35. The reference for each part is: OPT01 against the plain buy-write proxy and beta; OPT02 against the peer median; OPT03 against beta and a beta-matched mix.
- Fit view, with the current portfolio = 50 (the owner's mix at target until positions exist): FIT04 0.40, FIT05 0.20, FIT06 0.20, FIT10 0.20 for equity groups; FIT05, FIT06 and FIT10 at 1/3 each for gold, bonds, bitcoin and options-income.
- News view N, per group: 50 + 12.5 x the mapping- and confidence-weighted mean of the group scores from NWS01-NWS17 and EVT01, EVT02 and EVT04-EVT08 (range 25-75). EVT09 is logged but excluded.

6. Weights inside families:
- CST: CST01 0.30, CST02 0.35, CST04 0.15 (noise only until 20 snapshots and NAV history exist), CST06 0.15, CST08 0.05. From 3 fills, REC05's realised cost takes 0.10 from CST06.
- TAX: TAX01 0.4, TAX03 0.4, TAX05 0.2. TAX01's fund-level part is inactive where CST02 exists, and its non-US look-through component is recorded at 0 until the treaty table is verified. Its investor-level part (30% for US-listed lines) is always active, and UCITS lines read 0 bps on it. In a group made only of UCITS lines with CST02 present, TAX03 and TAX05 are renormalised to 2/3 and 1/3.
- DUR: DUR01 0.6, DUR06 0.4 (DUR02 is a gate).
- Line EVT: EVT01, EVT02 and EVT05 severities in equal thirds, penalty-only at 50/40/25/0.
- EXR: EXR06 0.4, EXR08 0.6.
- FAC: FAC01 0.12, FAC02 0.30, FAC03 0.28, FAC04 0.30.
- RSK: RSK01 0.35, RSK03 0.35, RSK05 0.30.  
Caps: the value cluster is 24.8% of X (cap 25%) and the drawdown cluster 14% (cap 15%).

7. Weight 0 (gate, context, constraint, record or derived): all ELG, MAC and REC pillars; BTC01; CON02, CON04, CON05; CST10; DUR02; EXR02, EXR03, EXR10, EXR11; FAC05, FAC06; RSK06, RSK07; OPT04; FIT01, FIT07, FIT08, FIT11, FIT12, FIT13; EVT04 and EVT06-EVT09 in the line view.

8. Verification and missing data.
- A pillar with an unverified source has weight 0 until a probe passes and is logged in CHANGELOG. Unverified in round 2: NWS14, ELG08, CST02, CST10 (full form), TAX03, DUR02, DUR06, EXR06, EXR10, CON04, FIT01, FIT12, FIT13, and REC01-REC03, REC05, REC06 and REC08-REC10.
- Unverified constraints and gates fail closed for buying: FIT12 blocks US-situs orders, FIT13 reads the bitcoin and options-income targets as 0, DUR02 blocks gold and bitcoin lines, and ELG08 blocks unconfirmed lines. The exception is CON04, which is listed as missing because it has no rows.
- A missing pillar drops out, and the family score becomes 50 + c x (mean - 50), where c is the share of the family's weight with data. Nothing is filled with 50.
- If the group reference lacks a pillar, the pillar is missing for the whole group.
- For news, an unsaturated pillar is capped at |1| and low confidence; a pillar failing the REC09 audit reads missing for that week; a verified null check reads 0.
- FRED is fetched one series per request with curl --http1.1, falling back to the cached last-good value with an age flag (missing beyond 45 days). Cboe files are fetched with curl -L.
- Missing pillars are listed weekly.

9. Point in time. Stress windows count only once they have ended plus 1 month. Ken French, FRED and BIS data use release lags. News scores count only from the ledger row frozen before Monday's open. MAC series that are revised need ALFRED vintages before any historical test.

10. Smoothing. Monthly line decisions use the median of 4 weekly readings. X for targets is the mean of the last 3 month-ends. N for testing is the 4-week mean. Weekly values are display only.

11. Promotion. A view may move money only when its exact pre-registered rule passes REC04 against the owner's mix. For news, REC08's single primary test must pass first; other news ICs are exploratory. Each new rule adds 1 to N_TRIALS. Every k, threshold, weight, MAC flag rule, news prompt and cap band is frozen under one config hash, and any change is a new trial.

12. Built-in checks. The run fails loudly if any of these breaks:
- the world scores 50 on every X family;
- FIT04 = 0 +/- 0.1% with zero tilts;
- each same-index group appears exactly once in every count and IC;
- CSPX, VUAA, VOO, IVV and SPY form one group, VWRA and VT one, and JEPQ, JEPQ.L and JEQA.L one;
- money-market lines fail ELG04, and leveraged, single-stock, SVOL, YMAX, ULTY and BTCI fail ELG03;
- the US buy-candidate count is 145;
- CST10 for a US-listed line includes 30% of its trailing ordinary distribution yield (Dividends only, de-duplicated); in fallback mode it reproduces VOO 35, VT 53, JEPI 273 and JEPQ 394 bps from the 2026-11-13 inputs, with JEPI compared to JEPI.L and JEPQ to JEPQ.L;
- a US-listed line within 10 bps of the best non-US line is never chosen;
- FIT12's V_us equals the sum over ELG01 classes c and d;
- no order takes V_us above US$54,000 or bitcoin above 5%, and no month-end leaves B above 5.0% without an S7 order;
- no order or split remainder is below US$340;

## Allocation method (how each deposit is placed)

Allocation runs monthly; the weekly run never moves money. The owner's mix routes the deposit, the caps and gates shape the order, and scores and news only advise until a gate passes.

1. Cash. C = 2,500 AED / 3.6725 - US$2 FX minimum = US$678.74. Carried cash, residue and distributions received net of withholding are added. If the MAC05 peg flag is on, conversion goes through the cheapest available channel (EVT08). The deposit is never skipped. Batching over k = 1 or 2 months is unchanged from v2.

2. Sleeves and targets (config/portfolio_v2.yaml; every change logged in CHANGELOG).
- Current file: world 70 (VWRA, then FWRA and ACWD as fallbacks once the owner has checked them), S&P 500 20, Nasdaq-100 10.
- Bitcoin, per 'bitcoin in at 5%': strategic_mix.bitcoin target 0.05, labelled speculative, hard cap 5% (FIT13). The other sleeves scale pro rata to world 66.5, S&P 500 19, Nasdaq-100 9.5 unless the owner writes another split. This is an owner mix change, not a tested rule. The owner may instead set a target of 4.5% to reduce trim churn; the cap stays 5% either way.
- Options-income, per 'options-income in': its lines enter the universe as candidates. The options_income sleeve target stays 0 until the owner answers an explicit prompt in the weekly report and CHANGELOG. The prompt asks for:  
(i) the target (a 10% ceiling is proposed, not owner-set);  
(ii) the underlying (S&P 500 premium income, Nasdaq-100 premium income, or global);  
(iii) which sleeve it is carved from.  
REC07 variant (b) is shown beside the prompt: BXM trailed the S&P 500 by 3.75 points a year and BXN trailed the Nasdaq-100 by 10.2.
- Gold (0 or 5%) and bonds (D of 0-20%) remain owner choices. Any satellite is 0% or at least 5%, except bitcoin, which is at most 5%.
- Config edits to make before the first v3 run, for the owner to confirm:
 - strategic_mix.bitcoin {target 0.05, label speculative} and options_income {target 0, underlying null, carved_from null};
 - positions_as_of 2026-11-13 (a zero start);
 - orderable migrated to the dict schema, with entries for BTCW.L, IB1T.L, IBIT, JEPI.L, JEPQ.L, JEQA.L, JEGA.L and QYLU.L for the owner to check in TWS;
 - ibkr_entity, w8ben_signed_on and us_commission_plan;
 - the etc_checks and btc_checks blocks.

3. Gaps (FIT01). g_s = target_s x (V + C) - value_s, where V is the portfolio value from the fills ledger. positions {} with positions_as_of is a valid zero start. Only positions with no as_of date, or one older than 35 days, send the deposit to the world line.

4. Order.
- All cash goes in one order to the sleeve with the largest positive gap whose best line passes every gate and can take an order of at least US$340.
- A capped sleeve's order is cut to its headroom: bitcoin to the FIT13 room, a US-listed order to the FIT12 room. If that headroom is below US$340, the sleeve is skipped this month and its gap is carried.
- If an order is cut and US$340 or more is left over, the remainder goes to the next-largest gap as a second order in the same session. A smaller remainder is carried as cash into next month's C.
- Otherwise a second order is placed only if cash exceeds one order by US$680 or more.
- At most 2 orders, each at least US$340, so the US$1.70 commission stays at or below 0.5% (CST08).
- If no line in the sleeve passes, the money goes to the next gap and finally to VWRA. The deposit is never skipped.

5. Structural events first. Any EVT01, EVT02 or EVT05 at severity 2 or more, EVT07 at severity 3, or a gate failure blocks that line at once. The group's next eligible line takes the order.

6. Line choice inside the sleeve's same-index group. The lowest CST10 among lines passing every gate wins, using the fallback (TERnow + TAX01 + entry) wherever CST02 is missing. A challenger must lead by 5 bps or more at 2 monthly runs, each on the median of 4 weekly readings (hysteresis). The old line is frozen and still counts toward FIT11's 6-line limit.  
A US-listed line may win only if all three hold:  
(a) its CST10, including 30% investor withholding on all ordinary distributions, is at least 10 bps a year lower than the best eligible non-US line (20 bps in FIT12's amber band), or the group has no orderable non-US line;  
(b) V_us after the order is at or below US$54,000;  
(c) ELG08 confirms US-ETF access, ibkr_entity and a valid W-8BEN.  
Cap space is scarce. Over time it goes to the line with the largest saving in bps per dollar, and first to exposures with no non-US alternative.  
Results on 2026-11-13 inputs:
- the world, S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 sleeves stay UCITS: VT about 53 against VWRA 24.6; VOO about 35 against CSPX 23.5; QQQM about 29 against XNAS 33.4, a 4 bps edge inside the hurdle;

The US$60,000 headroom therefore serves mainly US-only exposures and, if needed, a US spot bitcoin ETF.

- Money arrives only through gap routing toward the 5% target, in orders of at least US$340 sized so that B after the order is at most 5%. A smaller gap is carried. Below a portfolio of about US$6,800 there is no bitcoin buy, and the report says so.
- Warning at 4.5%; no buys at 5% or above.

- Line: a physically backed LSE ETP (ELG01 class e, a secured ETN, for example BTCW.L in USD) that passes DUR02 and ELG08, has a TER seeded from its KID, and is cleared by the owner's crypto-ETP permission. In practice the UCITS lines (JEPI.L, JEPQ.L, and the accumulating JEQA.L and JEGA.L) win, because US lines lose about 2.4-3.6 points a year to withholding.

## Sell rules

Default: hold, and steer with new money. Deposits fix drift before any sale. Every sale is logged with its rule, sources, cost and hold counterfactual (REC06), and needs the owner's confirmation, except S7, which the owner pre-confirmed by setting the 5% cap. Under UAE Branch A a sale has no tax cost; the costs are commission, spread and FX.

S0. Never a reason to sell:
- price trend or momentum (MAC09, FAC06);
- any score or rank (X, fit, O, N), any NWS score, or any EVT group score;
- drawdown depth, volatility, any MAC reading or MAC10 state;
- bitcoin drawdowns or crypto volatility (BTC01);
- an options-income line lagging its index in a rally, or cutting its distribution;
- the REC06 thesis verdict.  
Evidence: weekly top-5 rotation returned 5.71% and monthly 8.68%, against 11.85% for holding (2015-2026); selling the core on its own downtrend returned 10.02% against 11.85%; REC04 tilts lose to the mix.

S1. Structural exit of a line, completed within one month of primary-source confirmation. Buying stops at once. Triggers:
- EVT01 at severity 2 or more;
- a gate turning to fail: ELG01, ELG02, ELG03 (including reclassification to information-only), ELG04, ELG08 confirmed by the owner, or DUR02 (custody or ELN counterparty failure);
- ELG05 stale for more than 7 trading days, confirmed;
- EVT04 or EVT02 moving the line out of its group or strategy;
- EVT05 or EVT07 impairing 5% or more of NAV, including bitcoin custody loss or an ETP issuer default event.  
Action: switch the whole position to the group's best eligible substitute (DUR06, lowest CST10), never into cash. A US-listed substitute is allowed only within FIT12 headroom; otherwise the substitute is UCITS or an LSE ETP. For bitcoin with no eligible bitcoin line, sell into the most underweight sleeve. Positions above 10% of the target line's median daily traded value are split across sessions (CST06), and the switch waits while the aligned NAV discount is above 2%, for 60 days at most.

S2. Cost or durability switch. First stop buying and send new money to the substitute. Sell only if all three hold:  
(a) the held line's CST10 exceeds the best eligible same-group line's by 15 bps a year or more at 2 consecutive quarterly checks;  
(b) 10 years of the saving exceed 3 times the round trip;  
(c) less than half of the saving rests on one tax rule (TAX03).  
This covers a US-listed line made dearer than a UCITS line by withholding or fee changes (REC10 or EVT06 evidence), for example a US options-income line against a new UCITS twin. A durability flag (DUR01 small with outflows, or EVT05 severity 1) stops buying; a switch follows only if the flag holds for 2 monthly runs and a substitute exists.

S3. Drift beyond limits (score-tilt sleeves, band maxima, FIT07, FIT08, RSK06, RSK07, and the options-income limit in FIT13). Stop buying the sleeve. Sell only if FIT01 shows that more than 12 months of deposits would be needed to cure it. Then sell down to the limit only, at most once a quarter, and buy the most underweight sleeve in the same session. Bitcoin is governed by S7, not S3.

S4. Removing a sleeve from the mix (the owner sets its target to 0, including bitcoin or options-income). Freeze it: no new money and no sale. Deposits dilute it. Bitcoin is still trimmed under S7 whenever it is above 5%.

S5. Bond-share rebalance (only if D is above 0). Once a year, never while the world is more than 20% below its high (MAC09).

S6. Tax or legal change for the owner (EVT06 at severity 2 or more). Freeze all sales until an adviser review and a re-run of TAX01, TAX03, CST10 and FIT12. S1 and S8 switches that reduce US-situs exposure still go ahead, and so does S7.

S7 (revised). Speculative bitcoin cap at 5%, a true cap.
- The check runs at every month-end, after that month's deposit has been routed (no bitcoin buy happens while B is 5% or above, so new money dilutes B first).
- If B is still above 5.0%, sell the bitcoin group down to 4.5% of the portfolio in one order, on the cheapest-to-sell held bitcoin line. The proceeds are routed by FIT01 gaps in the same session.
- Each trim is at least 0.5% of the portfolio, so its US$1.70 commission falls below 0.5% of the trade once the portfolio exceeds about US$68,000. Below that the commission share is higher but stays a fixed US$1.70.
- The month-end check is the enforcement point. Price moves between month-ends can carry B above 5% for up to a month, and this is disclosed to the owner.
- A bitcoin sale is never made because of drawdown, news or volatility (S0). The trim is a fixed owner limit, so it is exempt from the cooling-off period and the drawdown guardrail.
- If the owner sets a 4.5% target, buys stop at 4.5%, the trim still fires above 5.0%, and it sells to 4.5%.

S8. US-situs cap. At a month-end with V_us above US$60,000 (or the cap as updated after EVT06), switch the excess, bringing V_us down to US$54,000, into the same-group non-US line within one month: a UCITS line, or an LSE bitcoin ETP for bitcoin. Lines with the largest withholding gap go first, split across sessions under the CST06 size rule. If no eligible non-US line exists, sell the excess and route it by FIT01 gaps. From US$54,000 no new US-listed buys are allowed (FIT12). S8 is a legal-exposure switch with the same exposure and no market timing, so it is exempt from the drawdown guardrail and the cooling-off period.

S9. Information-only products (ELG03, now including SVOL, YMAX, ULTY and BTCI) and excluded wrappers (ELG01: US partnerships, unsecured ETNs) are never bought. If one is found in the account, it is exited under S1.

Guardrails:
- a 30-day cooling-off and a re-run of the scores before any non-structural sale (S2, S3, S5);
- no non-structural sale while the world is more than 15% below its 52-week high (MAC09); S1, S6, S7 and S8 are exempt;
- missing, stale or unverified data never triggers a sale.  
The core world line is sold only under S1, S2 or S8.

### Practitioner: approve with conditions
