**Healthcare +1.** The Democratic sweep lifted Medicaid- and ACA-exposed names on Nov 4 (CNC +12.6%, HCA +10.3%) on expected delays to the OBBBA cuts. XLV hit an all-time high and fell half as much as SPY on Nov 5. Drug-pricing pressure (permanent Medicare negotiation, GLOBE MFN from Nov 30) was already known. Prefer XLV or provider ETFs over XBI. This overlaps the defensive rotation, so don't count it twice.

**Industrials -1.** Defense: the FY27 topline is likely about $1.1T against a $1.5T request, the NDAA slips to 2027, and the CR expires Dec 11 with 31.5-50% shutdown odds. Data-centre names: GEV and FIX fell, and Goldman expects build delays in Texas and Georgia. Offsets are ISM at 55.5 and munitions replenishment, which favours missile-defense names.

**Utilities/RE -1.** IPPs took the hit (VST -14% with a KeyBanc downgrade, CEG -8%) after the Texas and Ohio moratorium pledges and 40 of 41 ballot measures went against data centres. Fed hike odds near 75% add pressure. Offsets: Vistra beat and raised, the moratorium cannot start before Jan 19 and does not target signed ERCOT agreements, and REITs held up.

**Clean energy -1.** The Nov 4 rally reversed on Nov 5. Analysts say "for solar, nothing changes" before 2027 because of the veto and the 60-vote Senate threshold. FSLR, ENPH and RUN all have weak guidance, and the Section 232 tariff from Dec 4 raises costs. This was a policy-relief squeeze, not a re-rating.

**Red flags (sector-level):** VST/CEG concentration in utility ETFs; TAN/ICLN exposure to the Dec 4 tariff; ITA/XAR exposure to the Dec 11 CR.
