**What drove the week.** On Nov 5 the S&P fell 4.4%, the Nasdaq 5.5% and IWM 4.6%, and HY OAS widened 16bp to 348bp. Three things combined: the post-midterm backlash against data centres, Microsoft pulling back on its Oracle and neocloud capacity, and Oracle's $20B raise at 7-8.65% coupons. Fundamentals held up. Q3 S&P EPS growth is +31.4%, although the average beat was only 2.7%. Nvidia has guided Q3 to $108B. The selloff looks like multiple compression plus financing stress, not a collapse in demand.

**Scores**
- **us_large_broad -1.** Cap-weighted indices carry the shared AI-capex factor. A Dec 9 hike is about 75% priced. Nov 10 CPI and Nov 17 Nvidia are the next tests. Breadth is fine: RSP set a record on Nov 4. Do not count this separately from tech_ai_semis.
- **us_small_mid_value 0** (down from +1). Value beat growth, and payrolls and ISM are firm. Against that, IWM moved with the HY widening, hike risk weighs on leveraged small caps, and E&P constituents are hurt by the oil-premium unwind.
- **us_factor_dividend +1.** XLP, XLV, SCHD, NOBL, SPLV, USMV, VIG and RSP all set records on Nov 4, while MTUM and SMH fell about 20-24% over the month. Prefer quality-dividend and low-vol funds; VYM and DVY lag. This shares its driver with healthcare_biotech, so do not double count.
- **consumer 0.** Gasoline is down 33c in a month to $4.04 and payrolls rose 165k. But sentiment is weak at 51, gasoline is still $1 above a year ago, and the fuel relief reverses if the US strikes Iran. Retail earnings (HD Nov 17, TGT Nov 18, WMT Nov 19) will decide this.
- **financials 0.** XLF +1.2% and BIZD +1.1%: lender equity has not priced contagion from AI. New CoreWeave debt sits with bank syndicates. BDC non-accruals at cost are elevated and NII is falling. That reflects spread compression, not Fed cuts.

**Red flags**
- BXSL set its March dividend at $0.60, down from $0.77, and OBDC's base dividend has been cut, so BDC ETF yields will reset lower.
- Cap-weight concentration: prefer equal weight within us_large_broad.
