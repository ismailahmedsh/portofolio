# J07 Emerging markets ex-China: 2026-W45

| Bucket | Score | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| india | +1 | medium |
| em_asia_other | -1 | medium |
| latam | -1 | medium |
| em_broad | -1 | medium |

**Regime.** Two US shocks pulled in opposite directions:
- **AI selloff:** the midterm data-centre backlash and the neocloud pullback sent SOX down 12% on Nov 5. Korea (KOSPI -6% with a circuit breaker) and Taiwan (TAIEX -12% on the week, stabilisation fund authorised, record foreign outflow) were hit hardest.
- **Oil:** Brent fell from about $118 to $94-99 as the election lowered war odds, which helped importers.

**India +1.** FIIs bought a net Rs 45,960cr over Nov 2-6, Sensex had its best week in six years, and the rupee reached a six-month high. Iran reportedly exempted Indian ships from Hormuz restrictions. Limits on the upside:
- October CPI (Nov 12) is expected around 6%, after the RBI's October hike.
- Reserves are $69bn below their peak.
- The Russia-oil tariff waiver can be revoked at any time.
- OFAC sanctioned an Indian shipping network on Nov 4.

**em_asia_other -1.** The shock is US policy and positioning, not demand: TSMC guided Q4 up 14% and Korea's chip exports rose 259%, so the score is not -2. Indonesia carries an MSCI downgrade overhang that runs to June 2027.

**latam -1.** Brazil has a post-election fiscal premium: the real is down 8%, Copom stopped cutting, and debt is 82.9% of GDP. Banxico signalled the end of its cuts and copper is down 5%. LatAm was low-beta to the AI crash.

**em_broad -1.** The North-Asia hardware drawdown dominates the cap-weighted basket. Flows into India and Turkey moved money within EM, not new money. This is the same factor as tech_ai_semis, not an independent view.

**Red flags**
- **EWY:** moves to a 25%-capped index around Nov 9.
- **EWT:** short-sale curbs and a gap against home-market timing.
- **EIDO:** MSCI Standalone consultation; +0.68% premium.
- **TUR:** lira above 50/USD.

**Dates**
- Nov 10: TSMC sales, US CPI
- Nov 11: MSCI review, Korea exports
- Nov 12: India CPI, CBRT report
- Nov 19: SARB
- Dec 4: RBI
- Dec 9: Copom, FOMC

**Shared risk:** a sharp semis rebound or a US strike on Iran would reverse the India and Turkey gains and the em_asia_other losses together.
