**Japan (-1).** The Nikkei 225 entered a bear market on Nov 6 (-5.96% to 57,434.77). It lost more than 9% on the week, its worst week since March 2020. The losses came from AI hardware as US semis crashed after the midterms (SOX -12.3% on Nov 5). Weekly moves were SoftBank -20.8%, Kioxia -36.8%, Advantest -19.6% and TEL -13.2%. Topix held up better: it rose 1.2% on Nov 5 on lower oil and fell about 6% only on Friday. Two risks keep the score negative:
- SoftBank reports on Nov 10 with its CDS near a 4-year high and an OpenAI funding shortfall.
- A BoJ hike to 1.5% on Dec 18 is likely.

Prefer Topix or MSCI Japan trackers over the Nikkei.

**China (+1, low).** Beijing added market support: a third swap operation, a cut in the buyback relending rate to 1%, the PBOC pledge to back Huijin, and the State Council's Nov 6 call to stabilise the market. The trade truce looks set to roll forward rather than lapse:
- Bessent says it runs to January.
- USTR proposed extending the Section 301 maritime suspension to Jan 9, 2027.
- The US paused a blacklist rule.
- Xi and Trump meet at APEC Shenzhen on Nov 18-19.

Offsets: PMI 49.3, CSI 300 bear market, retail on the sidelines, HK tech falling with the US on Nov 6. China/HK tech trades as high beta to US AI sentiment, not as a hedge. Cut the score to 0 if the summit slips or the rare-earth suspension lapses.
