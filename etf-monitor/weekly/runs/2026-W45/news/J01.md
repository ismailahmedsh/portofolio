**Regime.** Two factors drive the week.
- **AI funding shock.** SOX fell 12% on Nov 5, and Asian markets fell into bear markets. Stress is in levered AI credit: Oracle priced 7-8.65% coupons, and CoreWeave bonds reportedly yield above 17%. IG and CCC spreads are flat.
- **Iran war-premium unwind.** Brent (Jan-27 contract) fell 13% to about $98. The 10y breakeven dropped to 2.23% and the 10y yield to about 4.85%, down 40bp in a month.

The Fed is still about 75% priced to **hike** on Dec 9. The BoE, RBA and BoC hiked, and the ECB signals December. Oil is the main relief valve.

**Changes.**
- **govt_bonds +1:** lower oil and breakevens, a soft core CPI nowcast (0.21%) and unchanged coupon sizes. Prefer the belly: Treasury dropped "at least" from its coupon guidance, which adds term-premium risk at the long end.

- **gold 0:** gold trades as a real-rate asset, with central-bank buying as a floor and hike odds as a cap.
