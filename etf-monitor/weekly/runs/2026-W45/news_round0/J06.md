## Japan (-1, medium)
- **AI rout:** the Nikkei 225 fell 5.96% on Nov 6 to 57,434.77, more than 20% below its June record, so it is now in a bear market. Reports put the weekly loss near 9%, the worst in more than six years. The selloff tracked the US chip crash after the Democratic midterm sweep, on fears of AI regulation and slower data-centre spending. SoftBank Group, Advantest, Tokyo Electron and Kioxia led the losses ([Reuters via Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447732/japans-nikkei-slumps-more-than-5-as-market-rout-deepens-on-ai-worries), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/japan-s-nikkei-breaks-below-key-60-000-level-on-ai-selloff)).
- **BoJ:** held at 1.25% on Oct 30 by an 8-1 vote. Ueda has "all but locked in" a hike to 1.5% in December, and the September minutes were hawkish. USD/JPY is around 157.5-158.5 ([Reuters via AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/analysis-boj-locks-december-hike-053516000.html), [FXStreet](https://www.tmgm.com/en/analysis/market-news/article/the-yen-rises-on-boj-minutes-with-ueda-and-us-nfp-on-the-friday-docket-202611052245)).
- **Earnings:** Toyota raised its FY net profit forecast to 3.57tn yen from 3.25tn despite US tariffs ([AP](https://wtop.com/world/2026/11/toyota-raises-profit-projection-for-the-year-as-sales-hold-up)).
- **View:** solid non-tech fundamentals, but the AI-hardware weight in the index and the earnings risk to those names make this a mild headwind.

## China (0, medium)
- **Data:** the official manufacturing PMI fell to 49.3 (consensus 50.0) and non-manufacturing to 49.7. LPRs are still unchanged and there is no new stimulus ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/chinas-nbs-manufacturing-pmi-falls-to-493-in-october-non-manufacturing-pmi-eases-to-497-202611012335)).
- **Rotation into HK internet:** on Nov 5 the Hang Seng rose 3.67% and HS Tech rose 5.55%, led by Alibaba, Tencent and NetEase, as money left AI hardware. Both indices gave back 1-2% on Friday, and A-shares were weaker ([The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347630/Hang-Seng-Index-surges-nearly-900-points-at-close), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-05/hong-kong-stocks-roar-back-as-investors-rotate-away-from-ai)).
- **Trade:** the US says the truce runs to Jan 10, 2027, but Beijing has not yet extended the rare-earth control suspension, which lapses Nov 10 ([Motley Fool, analysis](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/05/china-rare-earth-export-pause-usar-mp-materials/)).
- **IPOs:** a busy HK pipeline of chip listings from Nov 10-13 ([Caproasia](https://www.caproasia.com/2026/11/07/china-gpu-chip-company-suzhou-denglin-technology-hong-kong-ipo-to-raise-225-million-with-expected-ipo-listing-on-10th-november-2026-founded-in-2017-by-li-jianwen/)).
- **View:** weak domestic data and no stimulus are balanced by favourable flows and a stable truce, so the net is neutral.

## Red flags
- **china:** the rare-earth suspension lapses Nov 10 unless Beijing publishes an extension.
- **japan:** Nikkei-tracking ETFs carry much more AI/chip weight than Topix trackers.
