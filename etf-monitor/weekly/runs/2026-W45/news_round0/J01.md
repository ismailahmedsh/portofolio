# J01 Global macro and central banks: 2026-W45 (2026-10-31..2026-11-07)

**Regime: risk-off.** Central banks are tightening against the Middle East energy shock, and the midterm result set off an AI-led global equity rout. Falling oil and fiscal-restraint hopes are partial offsets. US activity data stayed strong, so the risk-off comes from rates and valuations, not a recession scare.

## Key facts
- **Fed:** held at 3.75-4.00% on 28 Oct (October background). The vote was 10-2, with Cook and Hammack wanting a hike. After payrolls, CME pricing put the odds of a December hike at about 77% (CNBC, 6 Nov).
- **US jobs (October):** payrolls +165k against about 59k expected. Unemployment 4.1%, average hourly earnings +0.4% m/m and +3.1% y/y. August and September were revised up by a combined 84k. U-6 rose to 7.9% (CNBC, 6 Nov).
- **ISM manufacturing:** 55.5, with prices paid at 75.2. Prices paid has been above 70 for nine straight readings (ISM, 2 Nov).
- **BoE:** hiked 8-1 to 4% on 5 Nov; Mann voted for 50bp. Staff see CPI near 5% in 2027 Q2. Several members signalled more hikes (BoE, 5 Nov).
- **RBA:** reportedly hiked to 4.85% on 3 Nov. This comes from secondary sources only and is not used in scoring. The ECB (2.50%) and BoJ (1.25%) held in late October but flagged December hikes.
- **Treasuries:** yields fell 4-5 Nov on the midterm outcome and haven demand, then rose after payrolls. On Friday the 2y was about 4.5%, the 10y about 4.86% and the 30y about 5.27% (CNBC).
- **Treasury refunding:** $125bn, with coupon sizes unchanged. Treasury dropped "at least" from its auction-size guidance, which hints at larger auctions in 2027-28 (Treasury, 4 Nov).
- **Oil:** Brent fell about 13% on the week, the biggest weekly drop since 2022, on hopes of US-Iran de-escalation (Rigzone/Bloomberg, 6 Nov).
- **Equities:** S&P 500 -4.4% on 5 Nov; Nikkei -6%, Taiwan entered a bear market, Stoxx 600 down over 1% (Euronews, 6 Nov).
- **Credit:** US investment-grade issuance hit a record $1.88tn year to date, about a fifth of it from hyperscalers (Reuters, 6 Nov). The HY spread (OAS) was 3.48% on 5 Nov.
- **Gold:** rose about 4.8% on the week from a one-year low of $3,870, then dipped to about $4,060 after payrolls (CNBC, 6 Nov).
