**Main driver: the US midterms took some of the war premium out of oil.** Democrats won both chambers, and Trump and Vance blamed the Iran war and gasoline prices for the losses. Brent fell 8.7% to $94.62 on Nov 4 (Oilprice.com) after peaking at $106.65 on Monday, then recovered to about $98 on Nov 5. On Nov 1, OPEC+ held its December targets flat for a third month (about 31.01 mb/d); its next meeting is Nov 29. JP Morgan flags a possible 1.1 mb/d surplus. Support comes from a fifth straight weekly US crude draw, distillate stocks below 100m bbl for the first time since 2003, and an SPR at its lowest since 1982. Eurasia Group puts the odds of renewed US strikes at 60%. Net effect is a mild headwind for oil.

**Gold: neutral.** Gold recovered from a one-year low of $3,870 (Oct 30) to about $4,100 (CNBC, Nov 5) as the dollar and yields fell. October payrolls of +165k, against an expected ~60k, pushed gold back to about $4,070. CME FedWatch puts the odds of a December Fed hike at about 66% (the Fed is at 3.75-4.00%). Poland reached its 700t gold target. Silver held near $52.

**Metals: mixed.** LME copper had its worst week since March, near $13,663/t, on concerns about AI and data-centre spending. Physical supply is very tight: SHFE stocks fell 24% in a week, and Shanghai spot premiums hit a record RMB 2,615/t (SMM). Lithium carbonate hit new lows (Mysteel).

**Broad commodities: mild headwind.** Energy drives most of it. Soybeans fell to an 11-week low on a record US crop and slower Chinese buying, and wheat fell to a six-month low.

**Red flag (bucket level):** a possible US refined-copper tariff is distorting regional stocks. COMEX-based copper ETFs may diverge from global fundamentals.

**Watch:**
- EIA, OPEC and IEA monthly reports in mid-November
- SHFE CU2611 copper expiry, Nov 16
- OPEC+ meeting, Nov 29
- FOMC decision, Dec 9
