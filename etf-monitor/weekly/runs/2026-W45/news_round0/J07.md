## What mattered
The US Democratic midterm sweep hit EM through two channels:

1. **AI and chip rout (Taiwan, Korea).** Markets feared US data-center restrictions.
   - KOSPI fell 5.28% on Nov 5 and 6.02% on Nov 6. A circuit breaker was triggered and the won weakened to 1,356.6 (Korea Herald).
   - TAIEX ended the week at 39,701, with the NT dollar at an 18-month low.
   - Taiwan had a record US$28bn foreign outflow in October, and the government ordered the regulator to stabilise the market (Taipei Times).
   - Fundamentals are intact: Korea's October exports rose 93.7% to a record, and chip exports rose 259% (Yonhap).
2. **Oil relief.** Hopes for US-Iran diplomacy took Brent from near $118 to about $93, then back to around $98 (Gulf News).
   - This helps India: FIIs bought a net Rs 45,960cr over Nov 2-6 (NSE data via StockPil).
   - India's October PMI was 54.7, with prices easing.

## LatAm
- **Brazil:** Copom held the Selic at 13.75% and ended its cuts. It raised its 2026 inflation projection to 5.6%, and the dollar hit its 2026 high against the real.
- **Mexico:** Banxico cut to 6.25% in a 3-2 vote, called it a "one-time adjustment", and raised its inflation forecast while the peso was weakening.

## Other
