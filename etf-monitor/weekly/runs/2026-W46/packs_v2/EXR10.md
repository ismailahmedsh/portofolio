# Pillar EXR10: Bond expected return (diversifier sleeve, context)
Definition: Yield to worst minus expected default loss by rating (AAA-A 0.05%, BBB 0.2%, BB 1%, B and below 3%, x 0.6), minus TER, plus Treasury roll-down at the fund's duration, plus hedge carry for a hedged class, in %. Ranks bond lines within the diversifier sleeve and informs the owner's choice of D. Never sets a target.
Source: iShares UK bond holdings CSV (verified, but it has no yield-to-worst or rating columns); iShares bond product-page weighted YTM and credit-rating fields (to be probed); Treasury curve from FRED DGS1-DGS30 via curl --http1.1, with cached fallback (unverified). Better when: higher. Informs: portfolio_mix.
