# Pillar FIT04: Expected alpha against the current portfolio (equity sleeves)
Definition: alpha_i = E[R_i - R_f] - beta_i,P x E[R_P - R_f], in % a year. E comes from EXR02, and E[R_P - R_f] = sum of w_k x EXR02_k.
beta_i,P = beta_i / beta_P, where beta_i is the FAC05 beta and beta_P = sum of w_k x beta_k (single-index model, one beta source). P is the core alone until positions exist.
With zero tilts and equal costs, alpha_i = beta_i x ERP - (beta_i / beta_P) x beta_P x ERP = 0 identically.
Applies to equity sleeves only, at 40% of their FIT view; for gold and bond sleeves it is context. It must be above 0 for a score-driven target (only after REC04 passes) and is shown as a warning on owner policy targets.
Source: EXR02; FAC05; owner positions (core-only fallback) (verified). Better when: higher. Informs: portfolio_mix.
