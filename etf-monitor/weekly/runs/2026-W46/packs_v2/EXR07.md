# Pillar EXR07: Currency carry (record)
Definition: Sum over non-USD currencies of unhedged weight x (local short rate - US short rate), in %, point-in-time. Computed only if rates of age 60 days or less cover at least 70% of the exposure's non-USD weight, from one consistent source. Otherwise it is missing for every exposure, VWRA included. Weight 0 and not an EXR02 input.
Source: Candidates: BIS WS_CBPOL policy rates (EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CNY, KRW to 2026-09/10; no Taiwan series); ECB data-api €STR (verified 2.442 on 2026-11-05); BoE IADB SONIA IUDSOIA (verified 3.7335 on 2026-11-04); Bank of Canada Valet. OECD 3-month EUR and GBP series end 2026-01. (unverified). Better when: higher (recorded hypothesis). Informs: record_for_testing.
