## Scope (pillars v3)
The agent tracks benchmark switches, provider consultations, capping-rule changes, special rebalances, country reclassifications, changes to the reference index of an options-income fund or to the bitcoin reference rate, and single-company events that move top-10 weights. It also keeps each line's benchmark-change history in the snapshot store.
Any confirmed change triggers an immediate recompute of ELG07 groups, FIT07, FIT08 and CON02 from fresh holdings. A change that takes a line out of its same-index group is severity 2 and opens S1.
Group score from -2 to +2:
- adverse severity 1/2/3 (for example a methodology change that raises concentration, or an index downgrade) gives -1/-2/-2 for the affected groups;
- a confirmed favourable change (for example a capping rule that lowers single-name concentration, or an upgrade into a broad index) gives +1.
