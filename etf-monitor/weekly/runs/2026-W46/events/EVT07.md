# EVT07: Sanctions, capital controls and market access (2026-W46)

**Verdict: no gate fires.** Every event is severity 0. No deposit is blocked, and there are no new rows for config/restricted_markets.csv (that file does not exist yet).

- **US-China truce extended (2026-11-09).** China's Commerce Ministry extended its suspension of the October 2025 rare-earth and critical-mineral export controls, and the 24% tariff suspension, to 2027-01-10. That removes the 2026-11-10 cliff. Beijing can reinstate the measures after January. This affects trade, not investor access.
- **US outbound-investment rule (COINS Act proposal).** Comments are due 2026-12-07 and the final rule by 2027-03-13. The proposal adds the 1260H list (Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, BYD, CATL and others) and Chinese AI and biotech to the outbound ban. Publicly traded securities stay exempt, and the owner is not a US person. There is no effect on UCITS index lines; watch for any index-provider response.
- **Taiwan.** October saw a record US$28bn foreign outflow and the central bank intervened, with reserves at US$596bn. No restriction on foreign trading or repatriation has been announced.
- **Indonesia (EM lines only).** MSCI proposes moving Indonesia from EM to Standalone status, with a decision by 2027-06-30. Indonesia is 0.43% of MSCI EM, so this matters for EIMI.L and IEMA.L only. FTSE kept Indonesia at Secondary Emerging, so VWRA is unaffected.
- **Russia.** Russia's weight in all indices is already zero. US waivers and talk of easing do not affect holdings.
- **Nvidia.** The board's probe into a reported foreign-intelligence effort targeting its CEO is a governance matter, not a sanction or a market-access issue. It belongs to EVT04 and EVT05, not EVT07.

Next checkpoints: the Treasury final rule; MOFCOM after 2027-01-10; FTSE's December review treatment of Indonesian securities.
