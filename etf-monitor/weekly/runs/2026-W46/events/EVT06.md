# EVT06 Tax and legal regime for the owner, 2026-W46

**Verdict: no adverse change. Nothing blocks a buy, and no S6 sales freeze applies.**

- **US section 871(m):** IRS Notice 2026-61, released 2026-09-21 and checked against irs.gov, extends the phase-in through 2028. Only delta-one transactions are in scope, and QDD relief runs to the end of 2028. The qualified-index exemption is unchanged, so the near-zero fund-level US withholding of the swap S&P 500 lines (SPXS.L, XSPU.L) is intact. Recorded as severity 0. TAX03 already charges for dependence on this one rule. Watch for proposed regulations next.
- **US estate tax and situs:** no proposal found. The non-resident alien exemption is still US$60,000. No section 899-style withholding revival was found.
- **Ireland-US treaty:** the 15% dividend rate for Irish funds is unchanged. The only "renegotiation" headlines found date from 2016.
- **Irish fund regime:** Finance (No. 2) Bill 2026 cuts exit tax from 38% to 35% from 2027 and adds ETF self-assessment, both for Irish residents only. Deemed disposal is unchanged. No effect on a non-resident holder.
- **Canada:** Budget 2026 (Nov 5) changes section 116 withholding from 2027 and broadens the trust-rollout denial for non-residents. Neither affects a non-resident individual's directly held UCITS ETFs. No change to s.128.1 or s.94.1 was found. Source is Osler's briefing; the Finance Canada annex has not been checked yet.
- **UAE:** no personal income tax move found. CRS 2.0 applies from 2027-01-01 as already announced.
- **PRIIPs / KID:** the UK Consumer Composite Investments (CCI) regime has applied since April 2026, with transition to June 2027. Not relevant to a UAE-resident client.

Coverage limit: search-based. Revenue.ie, CRA and UAE MoF news pages were not scanned directly.
