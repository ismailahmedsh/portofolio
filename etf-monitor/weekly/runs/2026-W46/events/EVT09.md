# EVT09 - Deposit-window calendar, 2026-W46

The window covered is about 13 Nov to 10 Dec 2026. All events are severity 0 (information only); none blocks or penalises a line.

## Key dates
- **17 Nov:** Nvidia Q3 FY27 results after the US close; the board's decision on CEO Huang is still open. US October retail sales at 8:30am ET.
- **18 Nov:** UK October CPI at 7am. FOMC minutes at 2pm ET.

- **9 Dec:** FOMC decision, with a hike near-certain per CME after the hot October CPI. The 10-year yield touched 5.03%.
- **10 Dec:** US November CPI.

## Market dislocation
SOX had its worst day since 2020 on 5 Nov, falling 12.25%. It has bounced about 17% but is still below its 200-day average. Nvidia governance risk is unresolved, so Nasdaq-100 and semiconductor lines face higher-than-usual gap risk around 17-18 Nov.

## Execution notes
- Do not place the order on 26-27 Nov.
- Do not place it within about 30 minutes of a US 8:30am ET release, or on 18 Nov before Nvidia's results have been absorbed.
- Use a limit order during overlapping LSE/US hours (14:30-16:30 London).

Event timing is not an approved signal under the decision rules, so the sleeve choice is unchanged.
